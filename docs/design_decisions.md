## Scope and question

- **Question:** How do scheduled local departure time, origin airport and reporting airline relate to flight reliability? The dashboard guides a reader from airport geography to weekday/hour patterns to airlines within a selected context.
- **Period:** Julyâ€“September 2025. A quarter keeps the data manageable and supports focused exploration. It cannot establish year-round or seasonal patterns. Earlier Januaryâ€“March work was retired after the quarter switch.
- **Geography for revised previews:** Contiguous U.S., Alaska and Hawaii **origin** airports. The earlier map had separate panels for Puerto Rico, U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, Saipan and American Samoa, with no land outlines. The student found it hard to read. The revised notebook reports excluded origin rows and uses the same scope for its map, heatmap, airline chart and totals. Destination airports are unrestricted. The immutable raw data retains all origins.
- **Metric:** Arrival-late means at least 15 minutes late. Its rate divides late arrivals by completed, non-diverted flights with known arrival status. Cancellation and diversion rates divide their respective counts by scheduled flights. Unknown does not mean on time, and zero eligible arrivals gives no rate. These rates have different denominators and should not be added together.
